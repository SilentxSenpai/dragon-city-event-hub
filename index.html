Follow pools and cooldown timers to finish laps faster without wasting gems.

Lap 1 - Warm Up No Pool Cooldowns

• Node 1: Collect Gold (x4) | Feed Dragons (x3)
• Node 2: Hatch Dragons (x3) | Breed Dragons (x2)
• Node 3: Win League Battles (x2) | Collect Food (x4)

• Strategy: Breed Terra + Terra for quick item drops.
